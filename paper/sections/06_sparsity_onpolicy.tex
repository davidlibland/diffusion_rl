\section{Where the training pairs come from}
\label{sec:06_sparsity_onpolicy}

Everything so far concerns the divergence applied to a pair $(x_t,q)$. This
section describes how we generate those pairs, because the choice interacts
with the loss more than we expected, and because the experiments of
\S\ref{sec:07_experiments} need it stated. It is a description of the tool we
use, not a claim about samplers; the comparisons behind the choices, including
the arms that failed, are in App.~\ref{sec:B_onpolicy_zoo}.

\paragraph{The two sources.}
Off-policy \emph{anchors} draw $x_1$ from the base terminal marginal and place
$x_t$ on the bridge to it (\S\ref{subsec:targets}). They are cheap and cover
the whole marginal, and by \eqref{eq:hmart} their targets are exact. Their
weakness is that $x_1$ is drawn without reference to $r$, so as the reward tilt
grows, the fraction of anchors carrying informative targets falls --- the
importance-sampling cost of $\E[e^{r}]$ under the base process scales as
$e^{\mathrm{KL}(\mathbb{P}^{\star}\|\mathbb{P})}$ \citep{chatterjee2018sample}.

On-policy \emph{sweeps} run twisted SMC under the learner's own control
$u=2a\nabla \Vf_\theta$ and form targets from it. By
\S\ref{sec:02_setting}(ii) the H-martingale identity survives any adapted
twist once the Girsanov ratio is applied, so this changes the variance of the
targets and not their mean, and the learned value is itself the optimal twist.

\paragraph{What we found we had to do, and what did not work.}
Two practical points are worth stating in the body because they run against the
obvious approach. First, \emph{sharpening} the twist toward the reward corridor
does not help at high dimension and reliably hurts, even using the analytic
value as an oracle twist; what helps is \emph{widening} coverage, by noising
sampled points backwards through \eqref{eq:backward-kernel} and attaching the
same target. Second, that gain is not a target-network effect in disguise:
subsampling each epoch back to the original regeneration cadence leaves it
intact. The trust-region machinery of \citet{wang2026tritsmc} was inert on our
learned twist. Details, the method zoo, and the per-arm ablations are in
App.~\ref{sec:B_onpolicy_zoo}.

\paragraph{One caveat that belongs here rather than in the appendix.}
Under a twisted sweep the backward-noising step is no longer exact. The
identity \eqref{eq:hmart} transfers a target to a noised point when that point
is base-distributed; under the twist $x_s\sim\mu\neq p_{\Base}^{(s)}$, and the
Girsanov weight of \eqref{eq:girsanov-rho} corrects the forward sweep rather
than the reversal. Carrying the accumulated ratio $\rho_{0\to s}$ as a row
weight would restore exactness; the runs reported here do not, so the expanded
rows are biased under the twist. We use the construction because it works, not
because it is exact.
